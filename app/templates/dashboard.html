THE PROBLEM

High protein is not enough
to make a good meat alternative.

A raw material can be rich in protein and still bind, hold water or emulsify poorly. Ingredients that work alone may work better, or worse, together, and their properties differ between varieties, states and processing. NaijaProtein AI brings that evidence together so you can test ideas before the laboratory.
